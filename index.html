About

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Southeast University, advised by Prof. Lin Cheng, and a CSC-sponsored visiting Ph.D. student at Georgia Tech, working with Prof. Srinivas Peeta.

My research develops space-time(-state) network models and mixed-integer optimization methods for urban delivery and low-altitude logistics. I am especially interested in time-dependent and stochastic routing, truck-drone coordination, charging infrastructure, and crowdsourced delivery.

Across research, open-source software, and teaching, I aim to turn rigorous optimization ideas into reproducible methods that are useful to both researchers and practitioners.

01

Research

Time-dependent & Stochastic Routing

Two-stage stochastic programming and decomposition algorithms for vehicle routing under time-varying travel times and uncertain demand.

Dynamic space-time networks · Benders decomposition

Truck–Drone Collaborative Delivery

Space-time-state formulations that coordinate trucks and drones while representing energy, payload, rendezvous, and dynamic no-fly constraints.

Branch-and-price · Branch-and-cut · Low-altitude logistics

Energy-constrained & Emerging Systems

Joint location-routing models for charging infrastructure, dedicated and crowdsourced delivery, and other sustainable urban logistics systems.

Space-electricity networks · Infrastructure planning
