I am a Ph.D. candidate in Transportation Engineering at Southeast University, advised by Prof. Lin Cheng. I am currently a visiting Ph.D. student at the Georgia Institute of Technology, working with Prof. Srinivas Peeta.

My research focuses on transportation optimization, vehicle routing and scheduling, space-time-state network modeling, and emerging delivery systems. I am particularly interested in optimization problems involving time-dependent travel, stochastic demand and energy consumption, truck-drone collaboration, crowdsourced delivery, and charging infrastructure planning.

Methodologically, my work combines network modeling with mixed-integer programming and decomposition algorithms, including Benders decomposition, branch-and-price, branch-and-cut, ADMM, and related large-scale optimization techniques.

Research Interests

Time-dependent & Stochastic Routing

Vehicle routing under time-varying travel conditions and uncertainty in customer demand, with an emphasis on dynamic space-time networks and stochastic programming.

Truck–Drone Collaborative Delivery

Space-time-state network formulations for coordinated truck-drone routing with energy, payload, rendezvous, and time-dependent operational constraints.

Sustainable & Emerging Delivery Systems

Joint location-routing optimization for charging infrastructure, crowdsourced delivery, low-altitude logistics, and other emerging urban delivery systems.
